Good to Know

Where is BOOMiiS Restaurant?

BOOMiiS Restaurant is at No. 47, Adjiringano Road, East Legon, Accra, Ghana.

What food does BOOMiiS serve?

Authentic Nigerian food such as amala with ewedu and gbegiri, egusi, efo riro, ofada rice with ayamase, jollof and fried rice and pepper soups, plus Proudly Ghanaian dishes like waakye, light soup, groundnut soup, palmnut soup and palava sauce with banku, fufu or omo tuo.

How much does a meal cost at BOOMiiS?

Main dishes cost about GH₵86–160: Omo Naija combos GH₵86–138, rice meals GH₵98–150, Proudly Ghanaian dishes GH₵106–120, main soups GH₵120–150 and stews GH₵126–160. Swallows such as eba, banku, amala and pounded yam cost GH₵8–35.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes. Use the reservation form on this page or send a WhatsApp message to 050 638 7636, and the team will confirm your table.

Does BOOMiiS offer delivery or takeaway?

Yes. Order on the online menu for pickup or delivery. Online orders are paid in advance with Mobile Money, and for delivery the delivery fee is paid to the rider when your food arrives. You can also order through Bolt Food.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. Online orders are paid in advance with MTN MoMo or Telecel Cash. The basket shows the exact amount, our MoMo number and your order reference; enter your transaction ID and send the order on WhatsApp, and we start cooking once payment is confirmed. When you dine in, you can pay with cash or MoMo.

What time does BOOMiiS close?

BOOMiiS is open until 10:00 pm. Call 050 638 7636 to check holiday hours.

Is BOOMiiS good for families and groups?

Yes. There is indoor dining, outdoor seating in the garden bar, a kids' menu and great cocktails, so it works well for family lunches, celebrations and group dinners.
